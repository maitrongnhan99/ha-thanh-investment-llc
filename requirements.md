# Ha Thanh Investment LLC - Website Requirements

## 1. Overview

Design a **simple, professional website** for **Ha Thanh Investment LLC**, a computer repair and technology services business.

| Field | Value |
|--------|--------|
| **Business** | Ha Thanh Investment LLC |
| **Address** | 40490 Vista Road, Hemet, CA 92544 |
| **In business since** | 2020 |
| **Phone** | 714-794-0974 |

**Primary goal:** Help customers understand the services offered and get in touch via **phone** or a **contact form**.

---

## 2. Design & UX

- **Style:** Minimal, trustworthy, professional
- **Responsive:** Mobile-friendly
- **Usability:** Easy to navigate and read
- **Conversion focus:** Clear paths to call **714-794-0974** and submit the contact form

---

## 3. Site Structure

The site should cover these core areas:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **About Us** | Build credibility; show who the business is and why customers can trust them |
| **Services / Products** | Describe service scope, main offerings, and how work is done |
| **Contact** | Phone, address, and contact form |

**Note on layout:** Requirements mention both (a) separate **About** and **Products/Services** pages and (b) a **single page** that includes About, Services, and Contact with credibility content and **2 images**. Either approach is acceptable as long as all content below is included and easy to find.

**Suggested navigation:** Home · About · Services · Contact

---

## 4. About Us Page / Section

**Purpose:** Establish credibility for a local tech repair business operating since 2020.

**Content should include:**

- Company introduction and trust signals (experience since 2020, location, focus on repair/tech services)
- Why customers should choose Ha Thanh Investment LLC

**Images (2 required on About or homepage):**

| # | Image | Description |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **Product / hardware** | Chip, motherboard, or other hardware close-up |
| 2 | **Team at work** | Team assembling or repairing computers |

---

## 5. Services / Products Page / Section

**Purpose:** Clearly describe **what** is offered, **who** it's for, and **how** the business works (scope, products, process).

**Primary focus:** **POS (point-of-sale)** systems and related hardware/services.

### 5.1 Core product & service lines

1. **Refurbished / recycled products**
   - Source used cases and components
   - Refurbish and reassemble
   - Deliver working units *(note: sold without brand labels or country-of-origin markings)*

2. **Assembly**
   - Custom assembly of computers and related equipment

3. **Repair**
   - Chip-level repair
   - POS system repair
   - General computer repair (laptops and desktops)

### 5.2 Additional services (general IT / repair)

May also include:

- Computer diagnostics and troubleshooting
- Laptop and desktop repair
- Chip repair
- RAM installation / upgrade
- Electronic device rework / fabrication
- Software installation
- Hardware upgrades
- Data backup
- Wi-Fi and network troubleshooting
- IT support for small businesses
